Privacy

Meloir has no server, no account of its own and no analytics.

Nothing about you is sent to the people who make Meloir. Everything below happens on your PC, or between your PC and the services named here.

Last updated: 11 November 2026

What stays on your PC

These are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Meloir:

  • Settings: your choices in Settings, such as the theme or the equalizer, in prefs.json, with a backup copy next to it.
  • History: the last 30 songs you played and your recent searches.
  • Stats: how long you listened, and your most played songs and artists.
  • Genres: the genre found for each artist, so it is only looked up once.
  • Error log: a short record of problems with saving settings, in errors.log.
  • Updates: a downloaded update, in the update folder, until it is installed.

Song lyrics that were found are also kept, for up to 200 songs, inside Spotify's own storage on your PC.

To delete them: in Settings, Clear removes your history, searches, Stats and genres. Uninstalling Meloir removes everything in the Meloir folder.

What goes to other services

Meloir only contacts these services, and only for the reasons given. Each one also sees your IP address, as with any website. Their own privacy policies apply.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhen
SpotifyWhat the Spotify app always sends, as you, signed inWhile Meloir runs, since Spotify does the playing
LyricsPlus, lrc.red, LRCLIBThe song's title, artist and lengthWhen lyrics are shown for a song not looked up before
WikidataSpotify artist ids and artist namesWhen you open the genre menu
Apple iTunesArtist names Wikidata doesn't knowWhen you open the genre menu
GitHubA request for the latest releaseWhen Meloir starts, and when you check for updates
DiscordThe song, artist, album, cover link, time and, if you choose, a link to Meloir on GitHubOnly with the Discord status turned on in Settings

The Discord status is shown on your Discord profile, so other people on Discord can see what you are playing. It is off unless you turn it on, and it clears when you turn it off, pause or close Meloir.

Your Spotify account

You sign in with Spotify's own app, on Spotify's own page. Meloir never sees your password. It uses the session the Spotify app already has, only on your PC.

Meloir blocks ads, which goes against Spotify's Terms of Use, and Spotify may suspend accounts that do it. Use it at your own risk.

Children

Meloir does not collect information from anyone, including children.
